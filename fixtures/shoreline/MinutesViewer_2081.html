8.  STUDY ITEMS

 

A.  Discussion of Ordinance No. 1050 - Increasing the Shoreline Transportation Benefit District Vehicle License Fee by Ten Dollars, for a Total Annual Vehicle License Fee of Fifty Dollars, as Provided in Shoreline Municipal Code Section 3.60.030 - Sponsored by Mayor Roberts (30 Minutes)

 
Staff Report - Ordinance No. 1050, Vehicle License Fee
 
Presentation - Vehicle License Fee
 
Kendra Dedinsky, Transportation Manager, and Joseph Brandstatter, Administrative Services Director, spoke about the proposal to increase the vehicle license fee by $10, as outlined in Ordinance No. 1050. The increase aims to address the funding gap for road maintenance, potentially generating an additional $415,000 annually. The current vehicle license fee generates approximately $1.7 million annually, split between sidewalk and road repairs.

The Council focused on the rising costs associated with deferred road maintenance and the necessity of proactive strategies to avoid escalating repair expenses. They discussed the impact of inflation on vehicle license fees and reached a consensus on the importance of maintaining streets to prevent potentially larger repair costs.
